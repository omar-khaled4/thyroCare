Understanding Your Thyroid

Basic Information

The thyroid is a butterfly-shaped gland located at the base of your neck. It produces hormones that regulate your body's metabolism, energy levels, and many other vital functions.

Thyroid hormones influence every cell, tissue, and organ in your body, affecting heart rate, cholesterol levels, body weight, muscle strength, skin hydration, menstrual regularity, memory, and more.

Types of Thyroid Diseases

  • 1 Hypothyroidism: Underactive thyroid that doesn't produce enough hormones
  • 2 Hyperthyroidism: Overactive thyroid that produces too much hormone
  • 3 Thyroiditis: Inflammation of the thyroid gland
  • 4 Thyroid nodules: Lumps in the thyroid gland
  • 5 Thyroid cancer: Malignant growths in the thyroid gland

Treatment Methods

Medication

Synthetic hormones for hypothyroidism or anti-thyroid drugs for hyperthyroidism.

Radioactive Iodine

Used to treat hyperthyroidism by destroying overactive thyroid cells.

Surgery

Partial or complete removal of the thyroid gland in cases of cancer or large goiters.
